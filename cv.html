Finshape is a great company! Towards the end of my studies, I became somewhat overwhelmed with writing my thesis. I ended up extending my studies by another year, which gave me the perfect excuse to do something practical alongside it. I joined Finshape, where I worked on online banking applications using the company's internal low-code tool for frontend development. The low-code team was young, supportive, and genuinely a joy to work with.

My typical day was a healthy mix of detective work and UI development:

  • Decoding requirements: Reading specs in Confluence, digging through Figma files, and pinging analysts, designers, and backend devs with tons of questions to clarify details and edge cases.
  • Component crafting: Building responsive banking screens and UI components using the internal react based low-code engine.
  • Git battle royale: Trying to merge my code before everyone else to avoid painful merge conflicts (spoiler: I lost that race more often than I'd like to admit, which at least gave me plenty of practice resolving merge conflicts).
  • Delivery & collaboration: Our routine included daily frontend syncs to divide tasks and unblock each other, plus twice-weekly cross-functional standups with the broader project team to report status, provide time estimates, and align on dependencies.
