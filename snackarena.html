Snackarena

A mobile app for ordering food and drinks at sports stadiums — designed as part of our User Interface Design course, so spectators never have to leave their seats or wait in long lines.

Problem & Concept

Attending live sports events is exciting, but getting food can be frustrating due to long queues and missed game moments. Our solution allows fans to order refreshments directly from their seats, ensuring they never miss key moments of the match.

How It Works

  1. Scan QR Code – Spectators scan the QR code on their ticket, which automatically loads their seat number and the stadium's food menu.
  2. Place Order – Users select their food and drinks from the real-time menu, which updates based on availability.
  3. Pay Online – Secure payment options ensure a fast and cashless transaction.
  4. Delivery to Seat – The order is delivered directly to the user's seat, allowing them to enjoy the event uninterrupted.

Lessons Learned: UI Design Process

  • Figma – Designing & Prototyping – Created high-fidelity wireframes, interactive prototypes, and user flows.
  • Design System Creation & Usage – Established a consistent design system for typography, buttons, and color schemes, ensuring scalability and a unified user experience.
